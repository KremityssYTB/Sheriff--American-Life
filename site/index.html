THE EXPERIENCE

MORE THAN A PATROL SHIFT.

Build your own routine in a living world. Go on duty, respond to incidents, drive the roads, explore local communities, or step away from the uniform.

01 / DUTY

LAW ENFORCEMENT

Patrol operations, traffic stops, dispatch calls, investigations and prisoner transport are part of the developing gameplay.

02 / WORLD

FLORIDA TO EXPLORE

Urban districts, small towns, highways and rural environments inspired by the state of Florida.

03 / VEHICLES

ON THE ROAD

Emergency and civilian vehicles, driving systems and a world designed to be explored behind the wheel.

04 / LIFESTYLE

LIFE OFF DUTY

Step outside the role of deputy, explore neighborhoods and experience the civilian side of the world.

05 / CONTROL

PLAY YOUR WAY

06 / MOBILE

BUILT FOR iOS

Mobile performance, readable interfaces and device compatibility guide development priorities.

THE SETTING

A STATE-SIZED AMBITION.

The long-term vision is a connected Florida-inspired world that expands county by county. Recognizable cities, smaller communities and the roads between them are part of the roadmap.

The locations shown are planned world destinations. They are not a claim that the entire state is currently playable.
